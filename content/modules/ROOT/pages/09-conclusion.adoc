== Workshop Summary

Congratulations on completing the RHOAI 3.5 Operations Workshop. Here is a recap of what you covered:

=== Module 1: Operations Guidelines and Patterns

You explored platform-as-code principles, the GitOps operating model with ArgoCD, multi-cluster management with ACM, security baselines with ACS, and secrets management strategies. You deployed the OpenShift GitOps Operator and toured an ArgoCD-managed RHOAI deployment.

=== Module 2: Data Stewardship, Users & Access Control

You modeled a data-stewardship organization on the platform -- data owners, data stewards, and data scientists per line of business (Lending, Insurance) -- configured the RHOAI Auth CR access restrictions, bound per-persona roles in line-of-business projects, created custom workbench roles, configured MaaS performance tiers bound to groups for the Lending model, and proved the RBAC decisions with persona-based access walkthroughs.

=== Module 3: Managing Models and Assets at Scale

You built and imported a custom workbench image, created a ModelCar OCI container, explored Kueue resource management concepts (ClusterQueues, LocalQueues, ResourceFlavors), and reviewed llm-d serving topologies for large-scale model deployment.

=== Module 4: Upgrades

You examined the RHOAI release lifecycle, inspected operator subscriptions and upgrade channels, learned about dependent operator pinning (RHCL), and reviewed MaaS version differences across 3.4, 3.5, and the upcoming 3.6.

=== Module 5: Observe & Debug

You explored built-in RHOAI monitoring, configured metrics export, reviewed the llm-d Perses dashboards, and previewed the MaaS external metering architecture coming in 3.6.

=== Module 6: Backups & DR

You deployed OADP, created backups of RHOAI project namespaces, restored resources, documented the CRD dependency graph, and discussed the current product gaps and opportunities for future improvement.
