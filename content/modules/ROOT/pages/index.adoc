== Welcome

This workshop covers day-2 operations for Red Hat OpenShift AI (RHOAI) 3.5. You will learn how to operate, secure, scale, upgrade, observe, and protect your RHOAI platform.

This workshop is designed for platform administrators and MLOps engineers who are deploying RHOAI 3.5 and need to operate the platform at scale in production.

It is designed to be a follow-up to the **RHOAI Deploy & Configure** workshop.

We want to enable you to understand some of the patterns, practices and tool that are available to you to operate a RHOAI platform. 

There is no single right way to do this and we really do not expect to be able to cover the entire space in this single workshop.

Hopefully you will walk away with a better understanding of the platform and some ideas for how you can start and improve your operations.

If all we teach you is how to "start well" on this journey, we will have succeeded.

== What You Will Learn

* **Module 1** — Operations guidelines and patterns: platform-as-code, GitOps, multi-cluster management, secrets management
* **Module 2** — Data stewardship organization model: data owners, stewards, and scientists per line of business, the RHOAI Auth CR, MaaS performance tiers bound to groups, and hands-on persona-based RBAC across the platform
* **Module 3** — Managing custom workbench images, ModelCars, model registry, Kueue GPU scheduling concepts, and llm-d serving topologies
* **Module 4** — RHOAI operator upgrade lifecycle, dependent operator pinning, and MaaS version management
* **Module 5** — Built-in RHOAI monitoring, metrics export, and MaaS metering (Dev/Tech Preview)
* **Module 6** — OADP-based backup and disaster recovery for RHOAI resources

== Who This Is For

Platform administrators and MLOps engineers who have already deployed RHOAI 3.5 and need to operate it at scale.

== Prerequisites

* Completed the **RHOAI Deploy & Configure** workshop (provides your running cluster environment)
* Basic OpenShift administration experience (projects, operators, routes, CLI)
* Familiarity with RHOAI concepts (workbenches, model serving, pipelines)

== Estimated Duration

4–8 hours total across all 6 modules.
