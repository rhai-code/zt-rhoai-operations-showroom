== Welcome

This workshop covers day-2 operations for Red Hat OpenShift AI (RHOAI) 3.5. You will learn how to secure, scale, upgrade, observe, and protect your RHOAI platform.

== What You Will Learn

* **Module 1** — Operations guidelines and patterns: platform-as-code, GitOps, multi-cluster management, secrets management
* **Module 2** — Identity provider integration, user and group management, persona-based RBAC with a wine quality classifier exercise
* **Module 3** — Managing custom workbench images, ModelCars, model registry, Kueue GPU scheduling concepts, and llm-d serving topologies
* **Module 4** — RHOAI operator upgrade lifecycle, dependent operator pinning, and MaaS version management
* **Module 5** — Built-in RHOAI monitoring, metrics export, and MaaS metering (Dev/Tech Preview)
* **Module 6** — OADP-based backup and disaster recovery for RHOAI resources

== Who This Is For

Platform administrators and MLOps engineers who have already deployed RHOAI 3.5 and need to operate it at scale.

== Prerequisites

* Completed the **RHOAI Deploy & Configure** workshop (provides your running cluster environment)
* Basic OpenShift administration experience (projects, operators, routes, CLI)
* Familiarity with RHOAI concepts (workbenches, model serving, pipelines)

== Estimated Duration

8–12 hours total across all 6 modules.
